    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: July 2, 2026

    At CAP Consultor, we take the protection of your personal data seriously. This policy explains what data we collect through this website, for what purposes, on what legal basis, and what rights you may exercise, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD).

    1. Data Controller

    Data Controller: Lluís Moxó Pérez-Sala (CAP Consultor)

    Address: Barcelona, Spain

    Contact email: contact@capconsultor.eu

    Website: capconsultor.eu

    There is no obligation to appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO). For any privacy-related questions, you may contact us at contact@capconsultor.eu.

    2. Data We Collect

    Through the contact form on the website, we only collect the data you voluntarily provide:

    • Name (required).
    • Email (required).
    • Company (optional).
    • Message / inquiry (optional).

    We apply the principle of data minimization: we only request the information strictly necessary to handle your request. We do not collect special categories of personal data.

    3. Purposes of Processing

    • To respond to your inquiry and contact you.
    • To carry out the initial diagnosis/audit you have requested.
    • To manage the commercial relationship as a potential client (commercial lead).
    • To conduct internal analysis of the inquiries received in order to improve our services (aggregated use, without disclosure to third parties).

    We do not use your data for automated decision-making that produces legal effects or similarly significant effects, nor for profiling. We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties for commercial purposes. We will also not send you unsolicited marketing communications: we will only contact you in relation to your inquiry.

    4. Legal Basis

    Processing ActivityLegal Basis (GDPR)
    Responding to your inquiry through the contact formConsent of the data subject (Art. 6(1)(a)), provided by checking the acceptance box.
    Managing the resulting commercial relationshipPre-contractual measures at the request of the data subject (Art. 6(1)(b)).
    Internal analysis to improve our servicesLegitimate interest of the data controller (Art. 6(1)(f)).

    5. Data Retention Period

    We retain your data for as long as the commercial relationship or mutual interest continues and, once it has ended, for the legally required periods necessary to address any potential liabilities.

    If your inquiry does not result in a commercial relationship, your data will be deleted within a maximum period of 24 months from the last contact, unless you request its deletion earlier.

    6. Recipients and Data Processors

    We do not disclose your data to third parties, except where required by law. The only provider that processes the contact form data on behalf of CAP Consultor, acting as a data processor (Art. 28 GDPR), is:

    Supabase — database hosting provider where contact form messages are stored. The instance is located in the EU region (eu-west-1, Ireland), within the European Economic Area. The processing is governed by Supabase's Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

    The static website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.); this provider does not receive or store the data you submit through the contact form.

    7. International Data Transfers

    Contact form data is stored on servers located within the European Union and does not leave the EU: no international transfer of the data you provide takes place.

    The hosting of the static website (GitHub Pages) does not receive the contact form data; any technical processing associated with serving the website is, where applicable, covered by the appropriate safeguards provided for under the GDPR (Standard Contractual Clauses and applicable adequacy frameworks).

    8. Minors

    This website and its services are intended for businesses and professionals. The use of the contact form by individuals under the age of 14 is not permitted.

    If you are under 14 years of age, please do not submit your personal data to us. If we discover that we have received data from a person under the age of 14 without the consent of their parents or legal guardians (Art. 7 LOPDGDD), we will delete it immediately.

    9. Your Rights

    You may exercise the following rights at any time:

    • Access to your personal data.
    • Rectification of inaccurate data.
    • Erasure ("right to be forgotten").
    • Restriction of processing.
    • Objection to processing.
    • Data portability.
    • Withdrawal of consent previously given, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to its withdrawal.

    To exercise these rights, please contact us at contact@capconsultor.eu, specifying the right you wish to exercise.

    If you believe that we have not properly handled your request, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at www.aepd.es.

    10. Security

    We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures based on the risks associated with the processing (Art. 32 GDPR) to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to the database through Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, and the principle of least privilege for data access.

    However, no system is completely infallible: no transmission or storage of data can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. If a security breach occurs that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify it in accordance with Articles 33 and 34 of the GDPR.

    11. Cookies

    This website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, nor third-party tools such as Google Analytics or social media pixels.

    It may only use cookies or technical storage mechanisms that are strictly necessary for the operation of the website, which are exempt from the consent requirement under Article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE.

    For this reason, no cookie banner is displayed and there is no separate cookie policy.

    12. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this policy to reflect changes in regulations or our services. We will always publish the current version on this page, together with its update date.
