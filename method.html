    METHOD

    A clear method, from idea
    to working system.

    Three phases built with Claude and n8n. First we understand the business, then we build, then we keep improving.

    01 — Diagnose & identify growth opportunities.

    We conduct a comprehensive analysis of your business model, market positioning, customer journey, sales funnel, marketing performance, operational structure, and financial data. We identify revenue leaks, inefficiencies, untapped customer segments, and the highest-impact opportunities for growth.

    02 — Build & implement revenue strategies.

    We transform our findings into a tailored growth strategy focused on increasing customer acquisition, improving conversion rates, maximizing customer lifetime value, and creating new revenue streams. We then implement the necessary marketing, sales, technology, and AI-driven solutions to turn strategy into measurable results.

    03 — Optimize, automate & scale.

    We continuously monitor performance through key revenue and growth metrics, identify what is working and what is not, and optimize every stage of the business. By automating repetitive processes and scaling the most profitable strategies, we help your company increase revenue efficiently while building a more scalable and sustainable operation.
